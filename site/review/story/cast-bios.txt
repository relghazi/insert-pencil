CAST BIOS
(In the comic only ACE is named. In the game, the who's who Polaroids in her locker show their first names, and their full names on the back.)

Ruby Ann Delgado (the hero)
Fifteen, a sophomore who fills every notebook margin with games she wishes existed. Left-handed, stubborn and patient, she would rather practice a run on paper a hundred times than waste a single quarter. When she hears the Quarter Moon is closing on Saturday, she has 75 cents, three days and one goal: put her initials above Ace's on the Defender board. Her pencil is the hero of the book as much as she is.

Adrian Cole Ellison ("Ace")
Seventeen, a senior, and the name on every line of the Quarter Moon's high-score board. The nickname is just his initials, which he chalked up so often that nobody remembers his real name. He acts bored by everything, but he has never missed a Saturday at the arcade. He is the first to say he saw her score.

June Park (Friend 1)
Fifteen, the smallest of the three and the driest voice in any room. She notices everything first, including the flyer that says the arcade is closing, and says it in as few words as possible. Practical about money, she is the one who can't believe Ruby skipped lunch for quarters. Under the deadpan she is Ruby's most loyal fan.

Theo Okafor (Friend 2)
Fifteen, on the JV team, cheerful and loud, and always eating something. He believes Ruby can beat Ace long before she does and tells anyone who will listen. He brings the crowd energy to the arcade on Saturday.

Harold "Hal" Moon (the owner)
Late sixties, has run the Quarter Moon since the cabinets were new. He keeps the scores in chalk on the old slate board and in pencil in a ledger, because a machine forgets and a pencil doesn't. Gruff over the counter, soft on regulars, never without the chewed, taped pencil behind his ear until he gives it away.

Mrs. Albright (the teacher)
Ruby's math teacher, seen only as purple sleeves, hands or from behind. Strict about notebooks in class, she confiscates Ruby's and gives it back with a quarter taped inside the cover. That quarter becomes the last quarter.
