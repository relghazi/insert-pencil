No. 2 Arcade v0.8.5

Open No2-Arcade-v0.8.5.html in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). It works offline. Its only network connections are the game's own online leaderboard, which you can turn off with "share my scores online" in settings, and a problem report when you send one from the detention slip.

CONTROLS
Locker: arrow keys move between the notebooks and the things in the locker, Enter opens, Esc returns to the title. The backpack shows the next ten notebooks. A controller works too: D-pad or stick to move, A to open, B to go back, and LB and RB turn a notebook's tabs (not during a game).
In a notebook: the five tabs are the title page, cheat sheet, play page, high scores and back page. Esc closes the notebook.
Scribble Defender: arrows fly, Space fires, Shift thrusts, R reverses, B uses a smart bomb, H warps, P pauses.
Erasteroids: left and right turn, up thrusts, Space fires, H jumps to hyperspace, P pauses.
Gym Class Pong: up and down move your bench, P pauses. First to 11 against the coach.
Detention Breakout: left and right move the ruler, P pauses. Three paper balls, two walls.
Space Substitutes: left and right move, Space fires a pencil, P pauses.
Lunch Lander: left and right turn, up thrusts, Space aborts, P pauses.
Spitball Command: arrows or the mouse aim, Z, X and C fire from the left, centre and right desks (Space or a click fires the nearest), P pauses.
Snack-Man: arrows steer, P pauses.
Recess Zone: up and down drive, left and right turn, Space fires, P pauses. On a controller both sticks drive the two treads.
Galgebra: left and right move, Space fires, P pauses.
Centigrade: arrow keys or WASD move around the bottom of the page, hold Space to keep firing, P pauses.
Froggology: arrow keys or WASD hop, P pauses.
Home Ec Scramble: arrow keys or WASD walk the girders and climb the ladders, Space shakes the pepper, P pauses.
Testpest: left and right move the claw round the rim, Space fires, Shift uses the superzapper, P pauses.
Quiz: arrows move along the edges, hold Space to draw fast or Shift to draw slow, P pauses.
Dunce Kong: left and right walk, up and down climb, Space jumps, P pauses. Four screens: 25 m, 50 m, 75 m and 100 m.
Q*Nerd: the arrow keys or WASD hop on the diagonals, turned like the cabinet's stick (up hops up-right, right down-right, down down-left, left up-left), P pauses.
Dig Doug: arrow keys or WASD dig through the dirt, tap Space to pump, P pauses.
Robotron: Class of 2084: WASD moves, the arrow keys throw in eight directions (two at once for diagonals), P pauses. On a controller the left stick moves and the right stick throws.
Jock Joust: left and right steer, Space flaps (one press each), P pauses.
Mystery Meat Time: arrow keys or WASD run and aim in eight directions, Space throws, P pauses. Reach the cone before it melts.
Hall Patrol: Left and Right (or A and D) slow down and speed up, Up or W jumps, Space fires forward and up at once, P pauses.
Term Paperboy: left and right steer, up speeds up, down brakes, Space throws a paper to the left, P pauses. Monday to Sunday, then the training course each evening.
Locker Gauntlet: arrow keys or WASD walk, hold Space to stand and throw, Shift rings a bell, P pauses. Health counts down; lunch brings it back.
With a controller: the play page waits for A. Scribble Defender: stick or D-pad flies, A fires, X smart bomb, Y warps. Erasteroids: stick or D-pad turns, up or RT thrusts, A fires, Y hyperspace. In both, Menu pauses (keep going, start over, cheat sheet, close notebook) and B goes back.
In the locker: the badges open achievements, the photo strip opens who's who, the score sheet opens your score notes (and every game's online board), the mirror adds up all your points, the sharpener on the top shelf opens settings, the pink eraser on the top shelf erases scores (with a second check), and the sketchbook replays the intro comic. The detention slip behind her sneaker sends a problem report.
